# Stay4OS Brand Specification

## Identity
- **Name:** Stay4OS
- **Tagline:** "Your phone. Your AI. Your rules."
- **Owner:** Stay4S (KvK 86200860)
- **Version:** 1.0

## Colors
| Element | Color | Hex |
|---------|-------|-----|
| Primary | Deep Ocean Blue | #0A1628 |
| Secondary | Electric Cyan | #00D9FF |
| Accent | Warm Gold | #FFB800 |
| Background | Midnight | #050A14 |
| Text | Cloud White | #F0F4F8 |
| Error | Coral Red | #FF4444 |
| Success | Emerald | #00C896 |

## Logo
- Minimalist "S4S" monogram
- Cyan gradient on dark background
- Geometric, tech-forward
- No Google-style colorful approach

## Boot Animation
- 5 second animation
- Black background
- Cyan "Stay4OS" text fades in
- Electric pulse effect around text
- "Your phone. Your AI. Your rules." appears below
- Fade to black, boot continues

## Wallpaper Pack (3 wallpapers)
1. Ocean Deep - gradient from #0A1628 to #00D9FF
2. Starfield - black with cyan star points
3. Circuit - subtle circuit board pattern in cyan on black

## Icon Set
- Rounded squares (not circles)
- Cyan accent on dark icons
- Monochrome system icons
- Custom app icons for Stay4S apps

## Font
- System: Roboto (AOSP default, familiar)
- Display: Inter Bold (for Stay4S branding)
- Code: JetBrains Mono (for AI chat interface)

## Sound
- Boot sound: soft electronic chime (3 notes, ascending)
- Notification: subtle digital tone
- Ringtone: ambient electronic melody
- No Google sounds

## Settings UI
- Dark mode default
- Stay4S branding in About page
- "Powered by Stay4S AI" in settings header
- Eigen AI status indicator (online/offline/model)
- WireGuard VPN toggle in quick settings

## Lock Screen
- Minimal clock (cyan)
- Stay4S AI quick access (swipe left)
- Camera (swipe right)
- No Google assistant
- Custom unlock animation (cyan ripple)
